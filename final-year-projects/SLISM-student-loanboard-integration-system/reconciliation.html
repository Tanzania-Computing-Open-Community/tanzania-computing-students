What counts as a verified match?

A consistent record supported by evidence from both sides.

01
Board evidence

Confirm the reported disbursement, reference and relevant period.

02
Receipt evidence

Verify the funds actually received by the institution.

03
Account posting

Confirm the receipt was correctly posted to the appropriate student account.

!

Important: Loan approval is not proof of disbursement. A reported disbursement is not, by itself, proof that the institution received the money.

FINANCIAL RECORD INTEGRITY

Keep funding status separate from fee-payment status.

The institution remains responsible for its student accounts, charges and verified receipts. The loan board maintains its own sponsorship and disbursement records. If a payment is reported but not yet confirmed by the institution, flag it for investigation rather than automatically marking the student as paid or indebted.
