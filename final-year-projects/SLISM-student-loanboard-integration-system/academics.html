DATA GOVERNANCE

Academic truth. Financial accuracy.

Student academic records, institutional fee accounts, and loan-board sponsorship records serve different purposes. They should be linked through verified identifiers without merging their separate responsibilities.

  • ✓ Academic results and progression are managed by authorised institutional staff.
  • ✓ Loan approval is not treated as proof of money received by the institution.
  • ✓ Financial balances are based on posted charges and verified payments, including confirmed loan disbursements.
