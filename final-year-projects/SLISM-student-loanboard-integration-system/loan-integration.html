DATA INTEGRITY & ACCOUNTABILITY

Keep institutional and loan-board records distinct

Integration should connect trusted records without merging responsibilities or assuming that a transfer has occurred.

▤

Institutional responsibility

The institution maintains admissions, academic registration, assessed charges, student accounts, and confirmed receipts.

Institutional finance →
◎

Loan-board responsibility

The loan board maintains its own sponsorship decisions, approved funding, disbursement schedules, and transaction information.

Independent funding records
⇄

Integration responsibility

Match authorized records, verify evidence, identify discrepancies, and send unresolved cases for reconciliation.

Review reconciliation →
✓
Critical rule: approval is not payment.

Do not mark institutional fees as paid solely because sponsorship was approved or a transfer was scheduled. Equally, do not treat a funding-related balance as a confirmed student debt without checking actual receipts, pending transfers, applicable deadlines, and institutional policy.
