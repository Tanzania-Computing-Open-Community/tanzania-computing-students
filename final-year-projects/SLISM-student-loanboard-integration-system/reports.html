Data integrity

Reports must reflect what is verified

A useful report preserves the distinction between institutional records and external funding records.

01

Institutional authority

The institution remains responsible for admissions, academic administration, fee assessment, student accounts and recording payments it has received.

02

Funding is not receipt

Loan approval and reported disbursement must not automatically be treated as payment received by the institution.

03

Exceptions remain visible

Differences, pending transfers and unverified transactions should be reported for review instead of silently changing student balances.

04

Protect student information

A production reporting service should enforce authorized access, minimize personal information and maintain an audit trail for sensitive exports.

✦

Designed for accountable reporting

Production reports must be generated from authenticated, authorized and validated data sources. This front-end prototype does not connect to a database or a live loan-board service.
