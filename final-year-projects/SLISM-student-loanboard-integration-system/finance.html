ACCOUNTING CONTROLS

Rules that protect student records

01

Assess charges independently

The institution maintains its own approved fee structure, assessments, invoices, and student account ledger.

02

Verify before posting

A payment reduces the recorded balance only after its receipt and allocation to the correct student account are verified.

03

Separate pending funding

Loan approval, scheduled disbursement, confirmed transfer, and money received are separate states that must not be confused.

04

Resolve discrepancies fairly

Where transfer or allocation evidence is incomplete, flag the account for review instead of automatically declaring default or marking it paid.

ⓘ
Prototype and data integrity notice

All names, amounts, and transactions on this page are illustrative. The current front end has no live database, verified payment gateway, or loan-board connection. Production use requires authorized data exchange, access controls, audit trails, and reconciliation procedures.
