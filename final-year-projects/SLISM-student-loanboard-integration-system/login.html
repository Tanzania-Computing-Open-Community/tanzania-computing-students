Data integrity principle

Funding approval is not payment confirmation.

An approved loan does not automatically mean that funds have been disbursed or received by the institution. Student accounts must reflect verified receipts, while pending funding and unresolved discrepancies remain distinguishable from confirmed unpaid balances.

✓ Verify actual disbursements and receipts.
