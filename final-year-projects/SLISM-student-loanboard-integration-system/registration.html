INSTITUTIONAL CONTROL

Registration and sponsorship are different records.

The institution controls admission and student registration. Loan-board approval and actual disbursement are maintained as separate funding information. A pending funding payment should not automatically cancel a valid registration or be treated as proof that institutional charges have been paid.
