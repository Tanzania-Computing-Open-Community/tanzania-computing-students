Important: prototype data and financial accuracy

This front-end demonstration does not authenticate students, connect to a live loan board, or retrieve official institutional records. The names, amounts, statuses, and notices are illustrative. In a production system, access must be authenticated and authorized, and financial status must be based on verified institutional receipts and reconciled funding records. Loan approval must never be treated as proof of disbursement or payment.
