# Artificial Intelligence Learning Resources

## Overview

This directory contains curated learning materials for **Artificial Intelligence (AI)**, covering the concepts, methods, tools, and practical skills used to develop intelligent systems.

The collection supports computing students, self-learners, educators, researchers, and open-source contributors across Tanzania through textbooks, lecture slides, research materials, practical exercises, laboratory guides, and project resources.

## Resource Categories

- **Books:** AI textbooks, reference materials, and study guides.
- **Lecture Slides:** Presentations and instructional materials.
- **Research Papers:** Academic publications and technical reports.
- **Practical Labs:** Hands-on exercises, experiments, and tutorials.
- **Assignments:** Problem sets, exercises, and assessment materials.
- **Projects:** Practical AI implementations and project guides.
- **Datasets:** Publicly available datasets for learning and experimentation.
- **Tools and Frameworks:** Learning resources for AI development tools and libraries.
- **External Resources:** Links to open courses, university repositories, and educational platforms.

## Learning Topics

Resources may cover:

- Artificial intelligence fundamentals
- Search algorithms and problem-solving
- Knowledge representation and reasoning
- Machine learning
- Deep learning and neural networks
- Natural language processing
- Computer vision
- Reinforcement learning
- Intelligent agents and expert systems
- Generative AI and large language models
- AI ethics, safety, privacy, and responsible development
- AI applications, evaluation, and deployment

## Resource Contribution

Contributors are encouraged to share accurate, useful, accessible, and appropriately licensed educational materials.

Where available, include:

- Resource title and author
- Publishing institution or organization
- Publication date or version
- Original source URL
- File format
- Intended learning level
- Topics covered
- License and redistribution permissions
- Required software, datasets, or dependencies

## Copyright and Licensing

Only upload materials whose licenses or copyright permissions allow redistribution. Free access or downloading does not automatically permit republication.

Preserve required attribution, copyright notices, license files, and other conditions. When redistribution is not permitted or the license is unclear, link to the original resource instead of uploading a copy.

For datasets and software, verify their respective licenses and usage restrictions before sharing.

## Contribution Principles

Contributions should promote practical learning, reproducible experimentation, technical accuracy, responsible AI development, and open collaboration. Organize materials into appropriate directories, use descriptive filenames, avoid unnecessary duplicates, and document sources clearly.
