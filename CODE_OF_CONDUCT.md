# TCOSC Code of Conduct

## Purpose

The Tanzania Computing Open Source Community exists to support learning,
technical collaboration, open-source development, research, and knowledge
sharing.

All participants are expected to contribute to a respectful and productive
environment.

## Expected Behaviour

Participants should:

- Treat others with respect.
- Communicate professionally.
- Welcome beginners.
- Provide constructive technical feedback.
- Respect different levels of experience.
- Give appropriate credit.
- Respect intellectual property.
- Follow project licenses.
- Avoid harassment and discrimination.
- Protect confidential information.

## Unacceptable Behaviour

The following are not acceptable:

- Harassment
- Threats
- Personal attacks
- Discrimination
- Intimidation
- Deliberate disruption
- Publishing private information
- Malicious activity against community infrastructure
- Plagiarism
- Deliberate copyright or license violations

## Technical Ethics

Cybersecurity-related contributions must be performed legally and
responsibly.

Do not use TCOSC infrastructure to attack systems without authorization.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove content, restrict participation, or take other
reasonable actions when community rules are violated.

Reports should be submitted through the appropriate repository security or
community reporting mechanism.
